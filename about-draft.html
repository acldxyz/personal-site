About

A pursuit of the absurd and truthful — fifteen years backing technology companies from the bank, the fund and the operator's seat, and now, mostly, fatherhood.

Tech banking at Piper Jaffray led to the buy-side at StepStone, then to Mousse Partners, a family office, where I invested in venture funds and companies. Privateer, an investment I led at Mousse, recruited me to lead strategic finance and complete a spectrum of public and private market transactions. I brought Delta Emerald, a data science-powered VC firm, onto the cap table through a fundraise, and later joined to lead internal research.

I became a father in November 2024 and took an extended break to spend time with my son. No other adventure has been as beautiful or absurd. I write about it at heirlloom.substack.com.

  3. 2019 – 22

    Privateer Holdings

    Principal · Seattle

    $60B of annual cannabis spend transitioned from the illicit market to the legal. The complex patchwork of new markets presented an ideal environment for purpose-built tech. Privateer had a portfolio of operating subsidiaries, across which I negotiated three mergers and raised over $100M.
